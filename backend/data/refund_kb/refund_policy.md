# Refund Policy

## Overview
Our refund policy balances customer satisfaction with fair business practices. All refund decisions should reference this document first. When in doubt, escalate to a senior agent or billing manager.

## Details

### Eligibility windows
- **Full refund**: Available within 14 days of the original purchase or renewal date. No questions asked.
- **Partial refund**: Available between 14 and 30 days from purchase date. Prorated for unused time.
- **No refund**: Requests made more than 30 days after the original purchase date are generally ineligible, except in documented service failure cases.

### What qualifies for a refund outside the 30-day window?
- Documented service failures: if our platform was unavailable or severely degraded for more than 24 consecutive hours during the customer's billing period, they qualify for a service credit or refund regardless of timing.
- Billing errors (duplicate charges, incorrect amounts): always refundable regardless of timing.
- Fraudulent charges (unauthorized use): always refundable; escalate to the fraud team immediately.

### What is NOT refundable?
- Annual plans cancelled after the 14-day window (unless service failure applies).
- Downgraded plans — the customer retains access until the end of the paid period.
- Add-on purchases consumed in full (e.g., API call packs already used).
- Plans purchased as part of a promotion where the promotional terms stated "no refunds."

### Partial refund calculation
For requests between 14-30 days:
- Calculate unused days: (30 - days since purchase) / 30 × monthly price
- Example: Customer purchased Pro ($99) 20 days ago. Unused = 10/30 × $99 = $33 refund.

## Process Steps
1. Verify purchase date in the billing system.
2. Determine which window applies (full, partial, or ineligible).
3. If ineligible, check for service failure exceptions.
4. Process refund in billing system and note the reason.
5. Email customer with refund confirmation and timeline.

## Edge Cases
- Accidental annual renewal: if a customer did not intend to renew and contacts us within 7 days of the renewal charge, we can offer a full refund as a goodwill gesture even if slightly outside the 14-day window. Manager approval required.
- Two active subscriptions: if a customer was accidentally billed for two accounts, refund the duplicate immediately regardless of timing.
