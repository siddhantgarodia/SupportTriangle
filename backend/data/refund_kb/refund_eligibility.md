# Refund Eligibility Guide

## Overview
This document helps support agents quickly determine if a refund request qualifies, and what type of refund to offer. Always cross-reference with the Refund Policy for edge cases.

## Details

### Quick eligibility check
Ask these three questions in order:

1. **When was the original purchase?**
   - Within 14 days → Full refund eligible
   - 14-30 days → Partial refund eligible
   - Over 30 days → Ineligible (check exceptions)

2. **Is there a documented service failure?**
   - If yes → Eligible regardless of timing
   - If no → Apply standard window rules

3. **Was it a billing error or duplicate charge?**
   - If yes → Always eligible, process immediately

### Eligible refund scenarios
| Scenario | Refund Type | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| New purchase, within 14 days, no usage | Full refund | Immediate approval |
| Annual plan renewal, within 7 days | Full refund | Goodwill; manager approval |
| Duplicate charge | Full refund of duplicate | Always eligible |
| Service outage >24 hours | Full or prorated credit | Escalate to billing manager |
| Mid-cycle downgrade | Prorated unused months | Calculate from downgrade date |
| Unused add-on pack | Partial (unused portion) | Only if < 10% consumed |

### Ineligible scenarios
| Scenario | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Annual plan, 45 days in, no outage | Outside 30-day window |
| Used 80% of API add-on pack | Mostly consumed, policy excludes |
| Promotional plan with "no refund" terms | Promo terms override standard policy |
| Monthly plan — customer "didn't know it would renew" | Renewal reminders sent; standard policy applies |

### How to communicate ineligibility
Be empathetic, clear, and offer alternatives:
- Mention the pro-rated period if they're just outside the window (it shows you checked).
- Offer to cancel at end of period to prevent future charges.
- Offer service credits as an alternative if the customer is frustrated.
- Do not say "I can't help you" — always offer a next step.

## Edge Cases
- Customers who paid via bank transfer and the payment bounced: do not process a refund — coordinate with the finance team to resolve the failed payment first.
- Customers who opened a chargeback: do not process a refund — coordinate with the dispute team to avoid double-refunding.
