# Dispute Resolution Process

## Overview
Some customers escalate billing issues to formal disputes through their bank or card network. This document explains how to handle disputes before they escalate, and what to do when a chargeback has already been filed.

## Details

### Pre-dispute resolution (preferred)
If a customer says they're "considering a chargeback" or "disputing this with their bank":
- Treat this as high-priority and respond within 2 hours.
- Offer to resolve the issue directly: refund if eligible, or explain clearly why it's not eligible.
- Most disputes stem from confusion, not fraud. A clear explanation often prevents escalation.
- Document the conversation in the ticket system in case evidence is needed later.

### When a chargeback has been filed
- **Do not process a manual refund** — this could result in double-refunding the customer.
- Notify the billing/finance team immediately.
- Collect evidence: subscription agreement, login records, feature usage logs, prior communication.
- Submit the evidence package through our payment processor's dispute portal within the deadline (typically 7-10 calendar days from notification).
- The customer's bank will rule within 30-90 days.

### Chargeback outcomes
- **Chargeback won**: funds returned to us. Customer's access remains active per their subscription status.
- **Chargeback lost**: funds remain with the customer. Close the account if they're delinquent, or work out a payment plan.
- **Chargeback reversed by customer**: customer asks their bank to cancel the dispute. Process as normal from there.

### Preventing disputes
- Ensure the billing descriptor on card statements reads clearly (not a generic company name).
- Send renewal reminders 14 days and 3 days before annual renewals.
- Make cancellation easy and visible — hard-to-cancel products invite chargebacks.

## Process Steps
1. Flag ticket as "dispute risk."
2. Attempt direct resolution within 2 hours.
3. If chargeback filed: escalate to billing team, freeze refund processing.
4. Gather evidence within 24 hours.
5. Submit via payment processor portal before deadline.
6. Update ticket with outcome when resolved.

## Edge Cases
- Second-time chargebacks from the same customer: escalate to fraud team, consider account suspension.
- International disputes: different card networks (Amex, UnionPay) have different dispute timelines and documentation requirements.
