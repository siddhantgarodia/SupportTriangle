# Subscription Tiers & Plan Management

## Overview
We offer three main subscription tiers: Starter, Pro, and Enterprise. This document describes what each includes, how to upgrade or downgrade, and how pricing changes are handled mid-cycle.

## Details

### Plan overview
| Plan | Price | Users | Storage | API calls/mo |
|------|-------|-------|---------|--------------|
| Starter | $29/mo | Up to 5 | 10 GB | 10,000 |
| Pro | $99/mo | Up to 25 | 100 GB | 100,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Annual billing offers a 20% discount on Starter and Pro plans.

### Upgrading a plan
- Upgrades take effect immediately.
- The customer is charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in the current billing cycle.
- Example: Upgrading from Starter ($29) to Pro ($99) on day 15 of a 30-day cycle costs approximately $35 (half the difference).
- New features and limits are available immediately after upgrade.

### Downgrading a plan
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
- The customer retains Pro features until the cycle ends.
- If usage exceeds the lower plan's limits (e.g., more than 5 users on Starter), the customer must deactivate excess users before the downgrade completes.

### Cancellation
- Monthly plans: cancel anytime. Access continues until the end of the paid period.
- Annual plans: cancellation stops renewal but does not trigger a refund (see Refund Policy).
- Data is retained for 90 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted.

## Edge Cases
- Grandfathered pricing: customers on legacy plans keep their rate until they voluntarily upgrade.
- Education/non-profit discounts: 30% off Pro plans. Requires verification via email domain or documentation.
- If an Enterprise customer downgrades mid-contract, contact the account management team — special terms may apply.
