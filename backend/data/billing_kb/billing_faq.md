# Billing FAQ

## Overview
This document answers the most common billing questions from SaaS customers. It covers invoice access, payment timing, and common billing misconceptions.

## Details

### When is billing processed?
- Monthly subscriptions are billed on the same date each month (the date of original sign-up).
- Annual subscriptions are billed once per year on the renewal date.
- If a billing date falls on a weekend or holiday, charges process on the next business day.

### Why was I charged more than expected?
- Mid-cycle plan upgrades are prorated: you pay only for the remaining days in the billing cycle.
- Seat-based plans charge per active user. Deactivated users are excluded from the next billing cycle.
- Add-ons (extra storage, API calls beyond plan limit) appear as line items on the invoice.
- Currency conversion fees may apply if the customer's bank processes in a foreign currency.

### How do I access my invoices?
1. Log into your account dashboard.
2. Navigate to Settings → Billing → Invoices.
3. Click any invoice to view or download as PDF.
4. Invoices older than 12 months are available via the "Archive" tab.

### Can I change my billing cycle?
- Monthly-to-annual switches are available at any time. The annual rate is applied immediately with a prorated credit for unused days on the monthly plan.
- Annual-to-monthly switches take effect at the end of the current annual period.

## Edge Cases
- If a charge shows as "pending" for more than 5 business days, the customer's bank may be holding the authorization. Instruct them to contact their bank.
- Duplicate charges within 48 hours of each other are almost always a bank authorization hold, not an actual double charge. Verify in the billing dashboard before issuing refunds.
- Free trial users are not charged until the trial ends. A charge on the trial end date is expected behavior.
