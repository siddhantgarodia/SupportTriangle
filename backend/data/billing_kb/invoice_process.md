# Invoice Process & Customization

## Overview
Customers frequently need custom invoice details for accounting or compliance purposes. This document covers how invoices are generated, what can be customized, and how to handle special requests.

## Details

### What information appears on an invoice?
- Company name and billing address
- Invoice number, issue date, and due date
- Itemized line items (plan name, quantity, unit price, subtotal)
- Tax breakdown (if applicable)
- Payment method (last 4 digits of card or bank account)
- Our company's registered business details

### What can be customized?
- Company name and address: editable in Settings → Billing → Billing Details
- VAT/Tax ID number: add in Settings → Billing → Tax Information
- Purchase Order (PO) number: add per-invoice in the invoice settings before the billing date
- Custom billing email: add up to 3 recipients in Settings → Billing → Email Recipients

### Process for updating billing details
1. Navigate to Settings → Billing → Billing Details.
2. Update the company name, address, or tax ID.
3. Click Save. Changes apply to the next invoice.
4. To update a **past** invoice with new details, submit a request with the invoice number — we can reissue within 60 days of the original invoice date.

### Tax exemption
- Customers with tax-exempt status must upload their exemption certificate in Settings → Billing → Tax Information.
- Tax exemption applies from the next billing cycle after certificate approval (typically 2-3 business days for review).
- Retrospective tax refunds for previously charged periods are handled case-by-case.

## Edge Cases
- If the customer's company was recently rebranded, we can update the name retroactively on invoices up to 90 days old.
- EU customers: VAT reverse charge applies automatically when a valid EU VAT number is added.
- Invoices in currencies other than USD are converted at the rate on the invoice date; we do not retroactively adjust for currency movements.
