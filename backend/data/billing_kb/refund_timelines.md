# Refund Timelines & Processing

## Overview
When a refund is approved, the timeline depends on the payment method and the customer's bank. This document provides accurate timelines to set customer expectations correctly.

## Details

### Standard refund timelines by payment method
- **Credit/Debit card**: 5-7 business days to appear on the statement. The hold is released within 3-5 days, but the statement credit may take 1-2 additional billing cycles to appear depending on the bank.
- **ACH bank transfer**: Up to 10 business days. ACH reversals take longer due to bank processing windows.
- **SEPA Direct Debit**: 5-10 business days.
- **PayPal**: 3-5 business days to the PayPal balance; additional time if the customer withdraws to a bank.
- **Wire transfer**: 5-10 business days, plus any correspondent bank fees.

### Annual plan prorated refunds
- Customers on annual plans receive prorated refunds for unused months if a refund is approved within the eligible window.
- Calculation: (months remaining / 12) × annual price, minus any applicable discounts.
- Example: A customer on a $948/year Pro plan (=$79/mo) who cancels after 3 months receives a refund for 9 months = $711.

### Partial refunds
- Partial refunds are processed to the original payment method only.
- Partial refunds for add-ons or overcharges are processed within 3-5 business days.
- If the original card is expired or canceled, a manual bank transfer is arranged (add 5-7 business days).

## Process Steps
1. Support agent approves refund in the billing system.
2. Refund is submitted to payment processor (same day).
3. Payment processor sends funds to issuing bank (1-3 business days).
4. Customer's bank posts the credit (2-5 additional business days).
5. Customer receives email confirmation when refund is submitted.

## Edge Cases
- If a customer's card was charged as part of a promotion and the promo price isn't honored on a retry, issue the difference as a manual credit.
- Refunds cannot exceed the original charge amount. If an overpayment occurred across multiple invoices, handle each invoice separately.
- International cards may take up to 14 business days due to international settlement processes.
